## Definition of the three panels (Glenn, 2 Nov: "I just scroll that section of the page, I don't lose all the other information")
- LEFT = the job (name, site address, stage, value, owner, key facts) and the contact(s) with phone and email. Everything click-to-edit with Save and Cancel on the same screen (InlineField).
- MIDDLE = the working area and the chronological history (calls, texts, emails, notes, tasks, visits, stage moves), newest first, with the composer (Note | Text | Email | Task) at the top. Leave a clear slot in a history item for an AI summary and a transcript (optional fields, rendered only when present; document it in a code comment).
- RIGHT = company, quotes (with status and Rev), linked people and companies, documents (links), invoices, pinned notes.
- Advanced = the full detail: all sections open, all fields, richer cards, counts and filters. Compact = same data, same editing, same shortcuts, but denser and calmer: sections closed with a one-line summary and a count, only key facts and the next action showing, short labels, one primary action.

## THE TWO NOTES (decided): make the difference obvious
- "Job notes" (left panel, one text, saved as the CRM field access_notes): what is ALWAYS true about this job (access, gate code, what the customer wants). Hint under it: "Always true about this job. New quotes start with a copy of these notes."
- "Add a note" (composer in the middle, dated list in the history): something that HAPPENED, with a date and an author. Pinned notes also show in the right panel under "Pinned notes". There is no other place to write notes on the job page.
